## Full Description

Liberty Guard is a comprehensive resistance and preparedness app designed to help activists, organizers, and citizens stay informed, connected, and ready to defend democratic rights.

## KEY FEATURES

• Real-time threat alerts and democracy health monitoring
• Live community messaging with secure encryption
• Protest message generator with 100+ impactful slogans
• Civic engagement and democracy checks
• Community organizing and mutual support tools
• Verified legal aid resources and safety guides
• Emergency readiness checklists and protocols

## STAY INFORMED & CONNECTED

Liberty Guard helps activists and communities share verified information, coordinate protests and actions, and strengthen preparedness during times of democratic decline. Whether you're organizing a march, monitoring authoritarian threats, planning local events, or staying aware of civil rights violations, Liberty Guard puts powerful tools in your hands.

## PREMIUM FEATURES

• Advanced threat intelligence with confidence scoring
• Live community messaging and coordination
• Protest message generator (random or by cause)
• Access to 35+ resistance survival guides
• Legal aid directory and rights documentation
• Emergency Beacon SOS with location sharing
• Protection checklists and preparedness tracking
• Offline guide access during network shutdowns

## BUILT FOR ACTIVISTS

Liberty Guard is designed for activists, community organizers, protesters, journalists, legal observers, and citizens who care about democracy, justice, and freedom. All features are built with privacy and security in mind—local-first architecture, AES-256 encryption, zero data selling, and minimal tracking.

• Empower grassroots organizing
• Provide accurate threat intelligence
• Support mutual aid and community resilience
